Sibling Discovery – SERP CSV Enrichment

Upload a SERP results CSV. For each YouTube row with a channel reference in handle (falls back to legacy likeness_match) and a title in client_category_override, the endpoint scans the channel for related videos and appends them as new rows. Appended rows also get normalized metadata (handle, duration, view_count, published_date, like_count, comment_count, description, tags) filled in wherever your CSV already has that column.
